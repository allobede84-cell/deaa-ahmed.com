👨‍🏫 Instructor Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about teaching with Edzo.

❓ What is Edzo for Instructors?

Edzo is an educational platform that enables instructors to create, manage, and sell online courses while providing students with a modern and organized learning experience.

❓ How can I create a course?

Simply create a new course, add its title, description, sections, and lessons, then publish it when it is ready.

❓ What types of educational content can I upload?

You can upload video lectures, PDF files, Word documents, PowerPoint presentations, images, and other educational resources.

❓ Can I organize my lessons?

Yes. Every course can be divided into sections and lessons to make learning easier for students.

❓ How do students join my course?

Students can join using a unique course code or by purchasing the course if it is paid.

❓ Can I manage my students?

Yes. You can monitor enrolled students, track their progress, and manage access to your courses.

❓ How do I receive payments?

Edzo provides a secure payment system that allows instructors to receive earnings from their published courses.

❓ Is my educational content protected?

Yes. Edzo uses security features designed to help protect your educational materials from unauthorized access.

❓ Can I update my courses after publishing?

Yes. You can edit lessons, upload new materials, and improve your course at any time.

❓ Can I teach from my phone?

Yes. You can manage your courses and communicate with students using your smartphone, tablet, or computer.

❓ Can I communicate with students?

Yes. You can answer questions, publish announcements, and provide learning support throughout the course.

❓ Why should I teach on Edzo?

Edzo provides powerful teaching tools, secure content management, student organization, and an easy-to-use platform that helps you focus on delivering high-quality education.
